Week 1 Discussion — Introductions & A Sense of Place

Original post due Friday, August 28, 11:59 pm CT. Two replies due Tuesday, September 1, 11:59 pm CT.

Prompt

Welcome to GHY 417/517. Since we won't meet in real time this semester, this thread is how we get to know each other.

Post one message with three things:

  1. Introduce yourself. Name, program (417 or 517), where you are physically this semester, and one sentence about what pulled you toward geography or environmental geoscience.
  2. Share a photo of a place that interests you spatially. This can be anywhere in the world — your neighborhood, a fieldwork site, a favorite hiking trail, a satellite image of somewhere you want to visit, a landscape you grew up in. Attach the image directly to your post (Canvas discussion editor supports drag-and-drop).
  3. Write one paragraph (~4–6 sentences) answering: why is this place interesting from a spatial or environmental point of view? What questions could a geographer or environmental scientist ask about it? Don't overthink it — first-instinct answers are the best answers here.

Then reply to at least two classmates. In each reply (≥80 words), do one of:

  • Suggest a specific quantitative or spatial method that might help answer the question they raised about their place.
  • Share a similar place you've encountered and what you noticed.
  • Ask a specific follow-up question that would help you understand their place better.

Please avoid "great post!" or "I agree, mine is similar." Aim for something that moves the conversation forward.

What I'm looking for

I'll read every post and reply to as many as I can, especially early in the thread. I'm not grading these on writing polish — I'm grading on engagement: did you actually look at your place with a spatial-thinking lens, and did you take your classmates' places seriously?

This thread also serves as the informal "community-building" moment for the semester. Async courses succeed or fail based on whether students feel like there are actual humans on the other side of the screen. Please take this seriously in the friendly, low-stakes way it's meant.

Timing (repeating deadlines you'll see all semester)

  • Original post: Friday of the release week, 11:59 pm CT.
  • Two replies: Tuesday of the following week, 11:59 pm CT.
